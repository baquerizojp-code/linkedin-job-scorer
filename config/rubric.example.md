# Job Scoring Rubric (Example Template)

> **This is an example template.** Tune the dimensions, weights, and red flags to your own job search. The wizard copies this file to `rubric.md` (gitignored) on first setup. See `HOW_TO_GENERATE_CV_AND_RUBRIC.md` for a prompt that auto-generates this from your CV and target roles.

Score each job 0-100. Sum of five weighted dimensions. Total weight must equal 100.

Suggested split (you can rebalance):
- Skills Overlap: 50
- Role Fit: 20
- Geo / Remote: 5
- Domain Fit: 15
- Company Signal: 10

## 3. Geo / Remote (5 pts)

- Fully remote, no geographic exclusion that would block your country: **5**
- Fully remote, but explicitly requires the candidate to live in a specific other country: **0** + RED FLAG
- Hybrid (any in-office requirement): **0** + RED FLAG (or adjust if hybrid is acceptable)
- Onsite: **0** + RED FLAG (or adjust if you'll relocate)
- Remote with country restriction excluding yours: **0** + RED FLAG

Scoring instruction: If the JD says "Remote" without specifying excluded countries, default to 5. Do NOT penalize for ambiguity.

- [Bonus condition, e.g. specific language/region/vertical]: +3 bonus (still capped at 15 total)
- General overlap with your target industry: **8**
- Adjacent but not core domain: **4**
- Unrelated domain: **0**

---

## 5. Company Signal (10 pts)

IMPORTANT: Missing compensation in the JD is NOT a red flag on its own. Most jobs omit compensation. Only flag as a red flag if the JD explicitly states compensation below your hard floor.

- Comp listed at or above your target: **10**
- Recognized strong company (well-funded startup, established SaaS, well-known scale-up), no comp listed: **8**
- Comp listed between hard floor and target: **8**
- Unknown or unfamiliar company, no comp listed: **6**
- Comp listed below your hard floor: flag as red flag, score **2**
- Other red flags (no website, vague description, recruiting agency posting, expired): **2**

---

## Recommendation Mapping

- **Apply**: score >= 70
- **Review**: score 50–69
- **Skip** (do NOT write to sheet): score < 50
